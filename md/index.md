# Kelvin Ma

I am a Canadian currently living in California and working on the [Google Photos application](https://www.google.com/photos/about/). My role focuses on editing and on-device machine learning. Previously I was working remotely as a software engineer at [Codecademy](https://codecademy.com).

I graduated from the University of Waterloo with a Bachelor of Mathematics majoring in computer science. I'm currently doing a master's in ML/Computer Vision through [OMSCS](https://omscs.gatech.edu/) at Georgia Tech.

I'm interested in using technology to solve interesting problems by understanding them end to end. I'm happy to use whatever tool is best for the job, ideally with short iteration cycles and best-in-class dev tools.

When I'm not working or learning about tech, I hike, take [photos](https://www.instagram.com/kelvinm14/), [cook](https://kelvinhanma.com/cooking), and play [music](https://soundcloud.com/kelvin-ma23).
